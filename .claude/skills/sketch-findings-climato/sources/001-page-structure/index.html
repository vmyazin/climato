CLIMATE OVERVIEW

Both cities sit in the temperate-oceanic zone of northwestern Europe, but Paris is roughly 350 km inland from the Atlantic and runs noticeably warmer year-round. Paris averages a 24°C high in July; London tops out at 22°C in the same month.

Rainfall is comparable in volume but distributed differently — London's wettest month is October (69mm), while Paris peaks in May (63mm). Both are equally cloudy in winter; Paris gets noticeably more summer sun.

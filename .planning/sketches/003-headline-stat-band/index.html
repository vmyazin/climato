Four-up grid of stat cards. Each card shows the two city values as a slashed pair, with the differential rendered as a separate mono-label row. Familiar grid pattern from the existing Climato VariationA hero.

Notes

  • Strength: Familiar from VariationA. Quick scanning — values on top line, verdict beneath.
  • Weakness: "15.6 / 13.6" requires the reader to do the subtraction themselves. The delta line helps, but feels secondary.
  • Tone: Neutral and data-forward. No editorializing.

B. Wide Stat Strip

Each stat is a short verdict sentence rather than a number pair. Reads like editorial copy. The differential is the headline; raw numbers are de-emphasized.
TEMPERATURE
Paris runs 2°C warmer on average.
RAINFALL
Paris gets 4% more rain per year.
SUNSHINE
Paris sees 16% more sun per day.
OVERLAP
Both peak May–August — ideal for either.

Notes

  • Strength: Reads as English, not data. Conversion-friendly because the takeaway is pre-chewed for the user.
  • Weakness: Hides the raw numbers — users wanting specifics have to scroll to the tables. Less SEO surface (no temperature digits in the hero band).
  • Tone: Editorial, opinionated. Good when the comparison has a clear pattern (Paris wins most categories); awkward when the picture is mixed.
